# Semantic Meaning (Word Pairs)

Semantic similarity refers to how closely related two words are in terms of their meaning. Below are 5 word pairs and an explanation of their semantic relationship.

| Word 1 | Word 2 | Similarity Type | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **King** | **Queen** | Relational / Co-hyponym | Both are royalty titles, differing primarily in gender but sharing the same semantic "power" bracket. |
| **Bicycle** | **Car** | Functional / Co-hyponym | Both are modes of transport used for commuting, though one is motorized and the other human-powered. |
| **Fast** | **Quick** | Synonym | These words share nearly identical meanings and can be used interchangeably in most contexts to describe speed. |
| **Laptop** | **Keyboard** | Meronymy (Part-Whole) | A keyboard is a physical component or a "part" of a laptop, showing a structural relationship. |
| **Doctor** | **Hospital** | Associative / Collocation | Not synonyms, but highly related through context; a doctor typically works within a hospital environment. |

## Why Semantic Meaning Matters in NLP
In Natural Language Processing, understanding these relationships allows models to perform tasks like:
- **Search Expansion**: Finding "automobiles" when a user searches for "cars".
- **Question Answering**: Understanding that "Where is the physician?" is related to the location of a "doctor".
- **Sentiment**: Recognizing that "brilliant" and "fantastic" both contribute to a positive score.
